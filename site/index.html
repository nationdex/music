How it works

Private by design, simple to start

Add your music and Adi Music reads only the files you choose. Nothing is uploaded.

You choose the files

Click Import tracks or drop audio files onto the page. Only what you pick is added to the library.

Your tags become the library

Titles, artists, albums and cover art are read from your files and organised into tracks, albums and artists automatically.

Library data stays on this device

Tracks, playlists and settings are saved in this browser so the player reopens quickly and keeps working offline.

Questions

Good to know

Is Adi Music free?

Yes. It is free and open source. There are no accounts, subscriptions or ads.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Your audio files, tags and cover art are stored in your browser and never leave your device. See the privacy page for exactly which requests the app makes.

Which audio formats work?

MP3, M4A and AAC, FLAC, Ogg and Opus, and WAV, as far as your browser can decode them. Tags and cover art are read from ID3, MP4 and Vorbis metadata. You can also import a ZIP of an album.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the app loads without a connection, and your imported tracks play offline. Install it from your browser's menu to use it like a native app. Lyrics and Discovery need a connection.

Where do the lyrics come from?

Adi Lyrics, with LRCLIB and LRC Mux as fallbacks. Time-synced lyrics scroll with the song when a source has them.
