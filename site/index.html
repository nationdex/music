How it works

Private by design, simple to start

Add your music and Adi Music reads only the files you choose. Nothing is uploaded.

You choose the files

Click Import tracks or drop audio files onto the page. Only what you pick is added to the library.

Your tags become the library

Titles, artists, albums and cover art are read from your files and organised into tracks, albums and artists automatically.

Library data stays on this device

Tracks, playlists and settings are saved in this browser so the player reopens quickly and keeps working offline.

Sound controls

Built-in equalizer

Tune it for your headphones or speakers. Start with a preset, then fine-tune each band until the sound is right.

Listen at any speed

Getting started

Start listening in three steps

  1. 1

    Import your music

    Open the player, then choose Import tracks or drop audio files on the page.

  2. 2

    Browse your library

    Tracks, albums, and artists are organized automatically from your file tags.

  3. 3

    Set up what plays next

    Build a playlist, queue a few tracks, mark favorites, and press play.

Works in modern browsers on desktop, Android and iOS. Your files and playlists stay on your device.
