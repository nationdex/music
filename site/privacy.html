Adi Music / Privacy

Privacy

Adi Music has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. This page lists what it stores and which requests it makes, so you can check it against your browser's network tab.

What stays on your device

  • Your audio files, their tags and cover art, in your browser's IndexedDB. Files are read locally and are never uploaded.
  • Settings, playlists, favorites, history and your equalizer in the browser's local storage.
  • A copy of the app itself, in the browser cache, so it can load offline.

Clearing site data in your browser removes all of it. The site sets no cookies.

Requests the app makes

These go through this site's own server, which forwards them. The services see this server's address, not yours.

  • Lyrics. When a track plays, its title, artist, album and length are sent to Adi Lyrics, LRCLIB and LRC Mux to find lyrics.
  • Discovery. What you type in Discovery goes to YouTube (its Data API, or YouTube's own web API as a fallback) or to Apple's iTunes Search.

These go straight from your browser:

  • YouTube playback. Playing a YouTube track loads YouTube's player, which is governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Apple previews. 30-second previews load from Apple's servers.
  • Discord (optional). If you turn on Discord presence, the page talks to a helper program on your own computer, at 127.0.0.1. Nothing else leaves your device for it.

What this site's server keeps

Access logging is turned off. Lyrics and search answers are cached briefly so repeated requests are fast. The cache holds the public answers, not who asked.
